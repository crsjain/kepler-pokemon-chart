Option 1: The "Trophy Gold" System Staff UX Top Pick

Key Design Rules:
1. Rest Day (💤): The circle is completely removed. The sleeping emoji floats naturally on the striped background. Zero false affordance—Kepler knows it's off-duty.
2. Bonus Available (✨): Warm golden-dashed ring that pulses with anticipation.
3. Bonus Completed (★ +10 XP): A Solid Amber/Gold Trophy Ball with crisp white checkmark. It is solid filled (unlike uncompleted tasks) and looks like an earned gold medal!

Option 2: The "Great Ball" Pokémon Lore System ⭐ Implemented in Live App

Key Design Rules:
1. Normal Tasks: Standard Red/White Pokéball.
2. Bonus & Rest Overachiever Tasks: Award a true Pokémon Great Ball (Super Ball) with its signature royal blue dome, scarlet red wing tabs, white bottom half, and clean +XP badge.
3. Rest Tasks: Borderless floating `💤` on striped cell with no circle outline or button affordance.

Option 3: The "Solid Fill High-Contrast" System High Contrast Clarity

Key Design Rules:
1. Rule of Completion: Incomplete items are always hollow with a white interior. Completed items are always solid saturated colors.
2. Completed Bonus: Solid Emerald Green filled circle with bold white `✓`.
3. Rest Tasks: Same borderless floating `💤`.
